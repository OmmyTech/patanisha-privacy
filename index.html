Utangulizi

Patanisha ni jukwaa la mali isiyohamishika nchini Tanzania linalomilikiwa na OmmyTech. Sera hii inaelezea jinsi tunavyokusanya, kutumia, na kulinda taarifa zako unapotumia app yetu.

📱

Taarifa tunazokusanya

  • Taarifa za akaunti — jina, namba ya simu, barua pepe, picha ya wasifu
  • Taarifa za mali — picha, maelezo, bei, na eneo la mali unayoiweka
  • Ujumbe — mazungumzo kati yako na watumiaji wengine
  • Eneo — mkoa, wilaya, na kata unazochagua kwenye wasifu wako
  • Taarifa za kifaa — aina ya kifaa na mfumo (kwa kuboresha app)
🎯

Matumizi ya taarifa zako

  • Kutengeneza na kusimamia akaunti yako
  • Kuonyesha mali na madalali karibu nawe
  • Kuwezesha mawasiliano kati ya wanunuzi na madalali
  • Kuboresha huduma zetu na uzoefu wa app
  • Kutuma arifa muhimu kuhusu akaunti yako
🔒

Usalama wa taarifa

Tunatumia Firebase ya Google kuhifadhi taarifa zako kwa usalama wa hali ya juu.

  • Uthibitisho wa Firebase Authentication
  • Sheria za usalama za Firestore
  • Uhifadhi salama wa picha kwenye Firebase Storage
  • Mawasiliano yaliyosimbwa kwa njia ya SSL/TLS
🤝

Kushiriki taarifa

Hatushiriki wala kuuza taarifa zako kwa mtu yeyote wa nje. Taarifa zako zinaonekana tu kwa watumiaji wengine wa Patanisha kama jina lako, picha, mali ulizoweka, na eneo lako. Mazungumzo yako yanaonekana tu na mtu unayeongea naye.

⚙️

Haki zako

  • Kuhariri — badilisha taarifa zako wakati wowote kwenye wasifu wako
  • Kufuta akaunti — futa akaunti yako kabisa kupitia mipangilio
  • Kuondoa mali — ondoa mali zako wakati wowote

Watoto

Patanisha haikusudiwi kutumiwa na watoto chini ya miaka 18. Hatukusanyi taarifa za watoto kwa makusudi.

Introduction

Patanisha is a real estate platform in Tanzania operated by OmmyTech. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

📱

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, phone number, email, profile photo
  • Property information — photos, descriptions, prices, and locations of listed properties
  • Messages — conversations between you and other users
  • Location — region, district, and ward you select in your profile
  • Device information — device type and operating system (to improve the app)
🎯

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To show properties and agents near you
  • To enable communication between buyers and agents
  • To improve our services and app experience
  • To send important notifications about your account
🔒

Data security

We use Google Firebase to securely store your information with industry-standard protection.

  • Firebase Authentication verification
  • Firestore security rules
  • Secure image storage on Firebase Storage
  • SSL/TLS encrypted connections
🤝

Information sharing

We do not share or sell your information to any third parties. Your information is only visible to other Patanisha users, such as your name, photo, listed properties, and location. Your conversations are visible only to the person you're messaging.

⚙️

Your rights

  • Edit — change your information at any time in your profile
  • Delete account — permanently delete your account through settings
  • Remove listings — remove your properties at any time
👶

Children

Patanisha is not intended for use by children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
